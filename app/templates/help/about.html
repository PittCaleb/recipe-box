About the author

Written by Caleb Cohen (GitHub, @PittCaleb).

For years, my recipes lived in three PDF files on Dropbox: Recipes.pdf, Recipes2.pdf and Recipes3.pdf. About half of them were handwritten, and none of them were searchable. Finding a recipe meant scrolling through the files on my phone, then keeping the screen on the entire time I cooked. The files also held duplicates and, worse, conflicting versions of the same dish, with no way to tell which one was right.

One day I asked Claude whether it could read those files and turn them into a single Google Doc that I could clean up and search.

That request grew into this app. At first it ran on my laptop, which meant keeping a laptop open in the kitchen, an even worse experience than a phone or tablet. So I moved it to a home server, where everyone in the house could reach the same live recipes. The natural next step was to make it available over the internet, so I could use it away from home and share it with friends and family. That, in turn, meant supporting multiple users.

Once the site was where I wanted it, I tried it in a web browser on the TV, so I could follow recipes from the adjoining kitchen. TV web browsers are not built for this. So I asked Claude, “How hard would it be to create an Android TV app that uses the recipe app’s API?” Seventy minutes later, a polished app was running on the big screen.

I have since deleted the original PDFs and rely on this app entirely for my cookbook. As a safeguard, its export feature (Print cookbook in the menu) combined those three PDFs into a single, consistently formatted cookbook.

Thank you, and an invitation

Thank you for using the app. I hope you enjoy it.

The goal is not a single, universal recipe book with one shared library for the world. The app is designed for small groups and families who want to keep their own recipes together in one place.

Contributions are welcome. To propose a change, create a branch and open a pull request on GitHub. I would like to grow the project into something more complete and useful for others. I am open to new features of every kind and would be glad to have more contributors.
